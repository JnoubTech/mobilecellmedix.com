iPhone 16 Not Charging or Charging Intermittently – Charging IC and USB-C Power Delivery Faults

June 15, 2025 4 min read

With the iPhone 16 series moving further into advanced USB-C Power Delivery (PD) management, charging issues are increasingly tied to board-level circuitry rather than the charging cable or port itself.

When an iPhone 16 refuses to charge, only charges at a certain angle, or repeatedly connects and disconnects, the issue is often not mechanical wear of the USB-C port. Instead, it can be traced to the charging IC and surrounding power delivery network on the logic board.

The Role of the Charging IC

This system is responsible for negotiating voltage (5V, 9V, 15V, 20V depending on charger capability), regulating current flow, and protecting the battery and main board from overcurrent damage.

If the charging IC becomes damaged — often due to liquid exposure, power surges, or repeated use of low-quality cables — the phone may still "detect" power but fail to properly negotiate fast charging or maintain a stable charge. In some cases, the device will only charge when completely powered off or will cycle between charging and not charging.

Cost-effective repair: Apple typically addresses these issues through logic board replacement, which can be costly. At MobileCellMedix, we perform board-level diagnostics to isolate whether the fault lies in the USB-C port, charging IC, or power rails.

With microsoldering, we can often restore proper charging function without replacing the entire board — saving both cost and your original device data.
