iPad Air & iPad Pro Not Charging? It Could Be a Logic Board Issue

June 15, 2025 4 min read

If your iPad Air or iPad Pro suddenly stops charging, only charges when turned off, or takes hours to gain just a few percent of battery, don't assume the charging port or battery is to blame.

On many newer USB-C iPad models, we've seen an increase in board-level charging failures. The charging system is controlled by several components working together, including the charging IC and the memory chips that store important charging and power management information.

What Causes Board-Level Charging Failures?

When one of these components becomes damaged — whether from a power surge, liquid damage, impact, or normal wear — the iPad may stop recognizing the charger altogether or become stuck charging at extremely slow speeds.

Many repair centers and even Apple will recommend replacing the entire logic board or replacing the iPad entirely. While that certainly fixes the issue, it's often the most expensive solution.

A Better Alternative: Microsoldering Repair

At MobileCellMedix, we specialize in board-level microsoldering repairs. Instead of replacing an entire logic board, we diagnose the failed component and repair only what's necessary. This approach is significantly more affordable and, in many cases, allows you to keep your original data, apps, and settings.

Save your data: Unlike a full logic board replacement, microsoldering repair often preserves all your original data, apps, and settings.

If your iPad isn't charging properly, bring it in for a professional diagnosis before spending hundreds on a replacement. A board-level repair could save both your device and your wallet.
