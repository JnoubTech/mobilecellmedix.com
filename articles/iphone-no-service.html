iPhone Says "No Service"? The Problem May Be Inside the Logic Board

June 15, 2025 4 min read

Seeing "No Service" on your iPhone can be incredibly frustrating. You've tried different SIM cards, restarted the phone, reset network settings, and maybe even updated iOS — but nothing changes.

On many recent iPhone models, particularly the iPhone 13, 14, 15, and newer devices, persistent network issues can sometimes be traced back to the logic board rather than the SIM card or carrier. Tiny communication circuits on the board are responsible for connecting your iPhone to cellular networks.

What Causes This?

If these circuits become damaged from a hard drop, bending, liquid exposure, or component failure, the phone may lose its ability to connect to your carrier.

Unfortunately, many repair shops simply label these phones as "unrepairable" or recommend replacing the entire device.

Board-Level Diagnostics

At MobileCellMedix, we perform advanced board-level diagnostics using professional equipment to identify the exact cause of the failure. In many cases, a microsoldering repair can restore cellular service without replacing the entire logic board — helping you save money while keeping your original phone and data.
